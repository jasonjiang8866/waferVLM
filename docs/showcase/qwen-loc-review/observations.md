# Visual notes before the per-map prediction key was opened

One reviewer (Codex) viewed the lettered plate at the supplied rendering. The true class, the 7/3 group sizes and the purpose of the review were known. The letter-to-ID key and the predictions for each letter were hidden. Positions are relative to the visible wafer. Yellow denotes failing dies, blue passing dies and black the background. The interiors below describe visible patterns, without assigning a second class label.

| Case | Position and extent of yellow regions |
| --- | --- |
| A | Yellow cells are scattered across the disk. There is a denser patch below and left of center, with shorter lines and small islands elsewhere. |
| B | Much of the left half of an elongated wafer is yellow. The lower portion is nearly solid and the upper part is mottled. A fairly straight boundary separates this region from the mostly blue right side. |
| C | A narrow horizontal band enters from the left edge, thickens near that edge and thins toward the middle. The rest has scattered cells and small rim patches. |
| D | A narrow oblique yellow streak joins the upper-right rim and tapers toward the interior. Isolated cells and partial rim patches are also present. |
| E | One large, solid, roughly round yellow region occupies the middle and extends toward the right edge. Blue remains most visible on the left. |
| F | Mostly blue, with sparse yellow specks, a small tight island above and right of center, and a few cells near the lower rim. |
| G | Scattered yellow cells, with the clearest concentration on the right rim. Several smaller patches appear toward the lower rim. |
| H | Coarse yellow blocks form a branching cluster near the middle. Other blocks are scattered across the wafer, including along its rim. |
| I | A large irregular yellow patch fills part of the lower-right quadrant and touches the rim. The opposite half has relatively few yellow cells. |
| J | Sparse yellow cells down the interior of a narrow wafer, with small groups near the top and middle. No dominant solid patch is visible. |
