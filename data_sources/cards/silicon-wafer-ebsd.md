- Primary source: https://zenodo.org/records/7491388.

  https://kikuchipy.org/en/v0.11.5/reference/generated/kikuchipy.data.si_wafer.html

- 50 × 50 scan positions with 480 × 480 diffraction patterns, acquired using a
  NORDIF UF-420 detector. This is EBSD, not electrical wafer maps or defect masks.

  Measured expanded payload: **581,340,387 bytes**. Official loader reports ~581 MB; the collector enforces a conservative
  **650,000,000-byte expanded ceiling** and records measured ZIP metadata.
- Good projects: orientation / pattern-quality maps and self-supervised microscopy
  representation. Neighboring scan points are correlated; split spatially or by
  acquisition, not by random pixels.
