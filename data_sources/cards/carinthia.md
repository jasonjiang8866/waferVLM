# Carinthia — original SEM image family

- Primary source: https://zenodo.org/records/10715190 (v2, 2024-02-27).
- Creators: Corinna Kofler, Sabrina Strauß, Anja Zernig, Ernesto Lazaro Garcia,
  Michael Boxleitner, Beatrix Mayr, Isabell Dicillia-Kovatsch, Claudia Anna Dohr.
- License **CC-BY-4.0**: [metadata](../metadata/zenodo-10715190.json).
- 4,591 real SEM images, six classes, one production layer of unstructured wafers.
- `data.zip`: **133,840,870 bytes**, MD5 `457011cf9063e5a49751f33ea468309d`.
  `carinthia_dataset.html`: 2,603,942 bytes. Expanded size: not measured here.
- Status: **catalogued only**. Prefer [Carinthia-S](carinthia-s.md) for both
  classification and segmentation. Do not pay to duplicate this image family or
  treat the original and masked releases as independent evaluation sets.

Citation: Kofler et al. (2024). *Carinthia dataset*. Zenodo.
