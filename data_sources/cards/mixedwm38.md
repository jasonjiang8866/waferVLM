# MixedWM38 — mixed defect patterns

- Author repository: https://github.com/Junliangwangdhu/WaferMap.
- Paper: Wang et al., *Deformable Convolutional Networks for Efficient Mixed-type
  Wafer Defect Pattern Recognition*, DOI https://doi.org/10.1109/TSM.2020.3020985.
- >38,000 maps, 38 patterns: normal + 8 single patterns + 29 mixed patterns.
  Author says the release combines factory data and GAN-generated balancing data.
- 52 × 52 arrays: 0 outside, 1 pass, 2 fail; eight-dimensional labels indicate
  basic patterns. The author notes corrected **C7/C9** labels.
